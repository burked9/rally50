Commodore's Letter: Oliver Kennedy (1998)

Commodore’s Letter Published for Derg Rally 23 in 1998

Dear Friends,

It is with great pleasure and a special privilege that I welcome you all aboard Rally 23. I am confident that with your full co-operation, it will be another successful week of fun and enjoyable activity for all participants.

Rally activities offer us an opportunity to make new friends; free ourselves of the stress of the working world; to relax and have fun. I encourage you all to participate fully in the programme.

A special word of gratitude to the Branch Officers and Organising Committee for giving freely of their time over the year in preparing the programme for our enjoyment, and for being so supportive to me during the entire Rally preparation season; to all previous Rally committees and past Commodores for bringing the rally to its present recognition. Further Voluntary workers are welcome to help out in competition events during the week.

Once again we are most grateful to Gortmore Boat Club for use of the harbour facility for our unofficial start and to Kilgarvan Angling Club for Clubhouse arrangements for Cheese and Wine evening.

It is perhaps a fitting time for us to look at our lake; how we love it; fear it, respect it, abuse it, walk away from it in wind and rain and yet return to it with a crispness and a freshness as if for the first time. Please use the treasured waters of Lough Derg with respect over the Rally and encourage your crew to wear appropriate gear at all times.

I thank most sincerely our advertisers (our primary funding source) and all those whose efforts, both physical and financial, have made this Rally possible.

I look forward to meeting you all personally during the week and before we set the fleet a rolling I wish all skippers and crews a most enjoyable and safe Rally in the knowledge that we will continue to share together our love for Lough Derg.

Yours sincerely,

Oliver Kennedy
Commodore
