Rally 40 (2015): Gathering of the Commodores & Flag Presentation

At the 40th Lough Derg Rally (2015), a special Flag Presentation ceremony was held bringing together living past Commodores and their families to honour their service across the decades:

Commodores & Families Presented: Bryan Brislane (Rally 7 – Lady Sarah), Colin Becker (Rally 8 – Lady G), the Duffy Family (Natalie, Yvonne & Robert) accepting on behalf of Thomas Duffy (Rally 6) & Maureen Duffy (Rally 9 – Lotus Two), Francie Boyle accepting on behalf of Chris Boyle (Rally 10 – Christina), Joe Treacy (Rally 11 & Rally 18 – 4E), Tos Quinn (Rally 13 – Abigail), Les Saunders (Rally 19 – 41M), Eamon Egan (Rally 21 – Hawthorn), Oliver Kennedy (Rally 23), Tom Moore (Rally 24 – Jasmine), Antoinette Makim accepting on behalf of her husband Eric (Rally 16 – Sunset), Lana Michelle Treacy (Rally 26 – Crystal Heart), Geraldine Burke (Rally 28 – 68M), Nicky Coady (Rally 30 – My Little Boat), Cathy Dwane (Rally 31 – 74M), Claire Jordan (Rally 32 – El-Roi), Noel Griffin (Rally 33 – Ocean Froggie), Carmel Byrne & Family (Rally 34 – Desperado), Jimmy MacFarlane (Rally 35 – ParadijsVogel), Rory Winston (Rally 36 – Highlander), Michael Geraghty (Rally 37 – Affaja), Robert Lambert (Rally 38 – De Eems), David Brassington (Rally 39 – Peggy May), and Daniel Burke (Rally 40 – 77M).
