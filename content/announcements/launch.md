# Over half century of wake, water, and wonder.

As we prepare for our landmark 50th Anniversary, we are honoring two legendary Joint Commodores who have steered our Branch for decades. But the biggest celebration is the history you members helped write.

We are building a permanent digital archive to preserve every memory, photo, and story from the last 50 years. Help us bridge the gap from the first rally to the future.
