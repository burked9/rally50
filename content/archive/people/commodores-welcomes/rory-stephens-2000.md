**Commodore's Letter**

Dear Friends,

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you all to our Rally 25 / Millennium event. We have lots to celebrate this year as it is Lough Derg's 25th Rally and our combined Millennium Rally with all other branches meeting in Athlone.

I am very much looking forward to working with Commodore Patsy and her Committee, to ensure that Rally 2000 is a great success.

A very special welcome to those who have never entered a Rally before as you are the new blood of all future rallies.

Rally 2000 is all about people enjoying themselves over the 10 day holiday period and that includes the combined rally committee who have worked tirelessly and with great determination over the winter months to make this event happen.

As usual our sponsors have shown great support and for this we thank them sincerely.

Finally, I hope you have a brilliant week, enter and enjoy the competitions, and above all do so in a safe and freidnly manner. Looking forward to meeting you all during the week.

Yours sincerely,

RORY STEPHENS
