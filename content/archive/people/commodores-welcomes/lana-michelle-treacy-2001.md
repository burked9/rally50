**Commodore’s Address**

Greetings to one and all,

I would like to welcome one and all to Rally 26 and the real new millennium. I would like to extend a big welcome to any newcomers and to the usual suspects.

Rally time is a time of enjoyment and getting together with old friends and meeting new, but it's also a time to learn new skill or refresh old ones which help make boating more enjoyable and safe. But most importantly we're here for the inland waterways, keeping it safe and clean for everyone around.

I would like to thank the work put in by the committee over the winter and also the real rally committee which are all those people who volunteer themselves throughout the week helping with competitions, entertainment and so on, because without you there wouldn't be.

Finally I would like to wish everyone an excellent week, enter competitions and be safe.

Looking forward to meeting you all over the week,

I thank you all,

**Lana Michelle Treacy**
