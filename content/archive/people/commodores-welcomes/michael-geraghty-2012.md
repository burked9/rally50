# Commodore’s Message

**Hello and welcome to Lough Derg Rally 37!**

Lough Derg is a wonderful place to spend summer holidays and get some fantastic boating experience. If my memory serves me right I got my first taste of boating when my Dad, many moons ago, brought home a small wooden open boat, which we duly and painstakingly scraped, filled and painted to a lovely sea blue, it was then brought to Wexford and used in the sea. (Alas I think it was stolen in the end).

Several years later (I think about 40 years ago to the year) Dad was asked for help from a good friend of his Seamus Kerrigan who had bought the “St Patrick” and wanted some engineering help and assistance – My apprenticeship began!! I loved those early years on the Rallies, I think I can claim to have been on rally 2 (not sure about rally 1) does that make me old or what!! Year after year on the Lough Derg Rallies I had great opportunities to meet up with old friends, and make lots of new ones, to learn lots of wonderful new skills watery and other! I learned to helm an 87ft barge, sail a Wayfarer dinghy, paddle a canoe, swim a mile (Dromineer), drive a powerboat, handle a boat in rough weather out on the lake, and I hope there will be many more new experiences to come.

This year I chose the team “Ar ais go dti on t-uisce” – (Back to the Water) which I hope will bring the water based activities to the fore in rally 37. We will be trying to have as many fun water based activities for both young and old as possible. I ask you all to be a part of rally 37, have fun and most importantly be safe. The theme also reflects the return of many to the rally who like me spent many of their younger years here and are now returning with their families.

The Rally Committee have been very busy bringing together an event filled week with the opportunity for all to have a great holiday, to learn or experience something new and hopefully have fun doing it. I would like to thank my committee for their hard work and commitment to the rally this year. As this week is also a holiday time for myself and the committee, it is important that all ralliers give a hand to make it fun for all over the week. Volunteerism is a core part of the rally spirit, so if you can help from the little things like making sure the harbours are kept clean to giving a hand running an event, please don’t be shy.

I would ask parents / guardians to ensure all our “little ones” and “big ones” are safe and take care to wear their life jackets.

I wish you all a very enjoyable, safe rally.

**Michael (Mick)**
