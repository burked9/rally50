*Commodore’s Letter Published for Derg Rally 22 in 1997*

Dear Friends, 

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you all to this, our Annual Rally, on Lough Derg. I would like to extend a special welcome to those of you who are with us for the first time and a heartfelt welcome back to those of you who have been with us from the start. I look forward to meeting you all personally over the next few days.

I would like to encourage you all to participate in the competitions and events during the coming week, as it is a great way to meet people and you may even learn something new.

To my hard-working Committee I say 'Thank You' for your tireless efforts and support. Without your hard work there would be no Rally and that is something unimaginable. I would ask all the Skippers and Crews to be kind and patient with these poor souls over the next week and to lend a helping hand whenever possible.

I would like to thank all our Advertisers whose financial support enables us to run this great annual event.

Finally, I wish you all a safe and happy Rally Twenty-Two.

Yours sincerely,

**John Moore**  
*Commodore*
