Title: Tranquillity
Date: 2024-02-15
Category: People
Slug: archive/people/stories/tranquillity

*This article was extracted from the Rally 17 magazine. Author: Unknown.*

Boats and barges, barges and boats,  
Down on the river everything floats,  
Some of them ancient, some of them new.  
Built in Athlone or perhaps Killaloe.  
Some of them sailed e'er their pilots were born,  
Some took the water but yesterday morn.  
Rocking together in peace by the shore,  
Mooring ropes tightening and slackening once more.  

Boats and barges, barges and boats,  
Always the subject of strange anecdotes.  
Each an adventurer, each has its tale,  
Of running aground, or surviving a gale,  
This one could ride in the heaviest sea.  
Now she's at home by a freshwater quay.  
Pride of the Shannon, once driven by stream,  
That one's a house-boat, the idler's dream.  

Boats and barges, barges and boats,  
Painted with no one knows how many coats.  
Grey as the heron, or white as the swan.  
Calm silent water reflecting each one.  
Put twenty cattle together to graze  
One of them surely some trouble will raise  
Put twenty people together to dwell  
Lucky you'll be if they get along well  
But moore twenty vessels beside the quay wall,  
Just leave them alone as the night shadows fall.  
Strif and commotion and envy will cease  
Why can't we, like them, live together in peace?
