# More Rally Memories

For years the St Patrick was a fixture on Lough Derg Rallys and I’m delighted to say that after a four year absence we’re back. I’ve been doing the Rally for as long as I can remember and it holds a lot of great memories for me: my first time at the wheel of a barge, my first eskimo roll, even my first pint (or at least my first pint in front of my parents). But despite all of these firsts, when our illustrious commodore asked me to put a few words about my Rally memories down on paper my mind went straight to my last Rally, in 2007. The irony about this is – I can’t remember it. Not because it wasn’t memorable, but because for some inexplicable reason I found my head between a barge and a hard place and wound up in the intensive care unit of Nenagh General.

I’m sure some of you remember this little mishap, sure what would the Rally be without a little drama. Of course those of you who were there will remember that thanks to some quick thinking from my brother Brian I escaped largely unscathed and ended up with nothing more than a cracked tooth and a bruised ego to show for my clumsiness.

For the last five years I’ve found various excuses not to take the barge on another Rally but her turn as a stage for last year’s gig at Kilgarvan brought all the memories flooding back so this year I’m back on the horse, back in the wheelhouse and back on the rally.

The L plates are hanging in the window and my apprenticeship as skipper continues. I learned the hard way that paintwork is more easily fixed than your skull, so remember safety first – use your head, but not the way I did.

**Mark Shanley**
