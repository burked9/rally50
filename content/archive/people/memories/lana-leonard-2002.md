**Reflections**

The first thing that greeted me on my arrival to Dromaan late on Sunday was a gang of kids and teens so my suspicions immediately set in. When we got to the Heart we found her decked out in balloons, banners and confetti. No one owned up but we soon found the culprit not too far away on Jasmine, we're still cleaning up confetti out of our bilges.

Anyway the next day kicked off with some competition and a sailing lesson for the commodore's wife by the young Egans. That was a treat and a half another couple of lessons needed I would think and he'll be ready for around the world. We had the cheese and wine that evening by the side of the harbour, which gave me the opportunity to catch up with everyone I hadn't seen in a while. I was extremely nervous at the pending speech. 2 glasses of wine later the speech was a blur but I was delighted with my gift from the branch. It takes pride and place in my living room.

The next couple of days were spent going to Dromineer and Kilgarvan and the morning briefings didn't get any easier, my fear was turning into a phobia. The competitions were in full swing and the evening's entertainment was as always, top class. With the Table Quiz, Fancy dress and Talent as well as the Teens entertainment it added to the enjoyment of the whole week.

We got to Mountshannon where we had the Ditty competition up in the Bridge bar. It was a cosy night with no space wasted but a good laugh as always. I was presented with my Pooka from Eric and was mentioned more than once in a Ditty or two. The next day began the winding down of competitions and the preparation for the Dinner Dance and also the gripping fear of another pending 'speech' but one good thing was it was going to be the last.

Apart from the delay the evening went well and once the speeches and prizes were behind me we settled into a night with great entertainment and the drink and craic was mighty.

The following day, even though the weather was not in our favour, we eventually had our coffee morning some time in the afternoon with a few rough looking heads. When every one dispersed exhaustion set in and I didn't surface until the next day.

A big thank you to the rally committee and to everyone else who helped throughout the week, without your help it wouldn't have gone as smoothly as it did. I also want to wish Andy and Cathy the best of luck on their week and the same to the committee.
