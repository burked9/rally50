Summary: Photos from the 40th Lough Derg Rally (2015), where past Commodores and their families gathered in chronological order for a special Flag Presentation ceremony.

At the **40th Lough Derg Rally (2015)**, a special Flag Presentation ceremony was held bringing together all living past Commodores and their families. The photos below are presented in the chronological order in which each Commodore served.
