Summary: A gallery of mysterious Pookas from the 90s.

The Púca (or Pooka) is a creature of Celtic folklore, famously known as a shapeshifter that could take the form of a black horse, goat, or rabbit. Often considered a trickster, the Púca was known to bring both good and bad fortune to rural communities. It is said that the Púca could speak with a human voice and would often take weary travelers on wild, terrifying rides across the countryside. Associated with Samhain (Halloween), it was traditional to leave a share of the crop for the Púca to ensure its favor. 

These specific Pookas, however, are mysterious artifacts from the 90s, their origins lost to time...
