# About the Committee

The IWAI Lough Derg Rally 50 is organized by a dedicated committee of around 12 volunteers who are passionate about the event and the Lough Derg region, wanted to stand beside and celebrate our two commodores for the upcoming event.

**More information about the committee members will be added here providing details on their roles and backgrounds.**

We are working hard to make the 2027 Rally an unforgettable event for everyone involved.
