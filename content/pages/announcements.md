<p>We are building a permanent digital archive to preserve the legacy of the Lough Derg Rally, but we can't do it without you.</p>

<p>Whether it’s old photographs, event brochures, physical artifacts like plaques and mugs, or simply a story from years past, we want to hear from you. Your contributions will help us paint a complete picture of the last half century of wake, water, and wonder.</p>

<p>Please reach out to us to share your piece of history.</p>
