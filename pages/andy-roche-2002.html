Rally 27 (2002): Andy Roche

Commodore's Letter

A Chairde,

Fáilte romhaibh go léir go Rally 27.

A very warm welcome to Rally 27 - to old(!) faces and especially those who join us for the first time. For those of you on your first Derg Rally I hope you will make many new friends and that this will be the start of a long association with Lough Derg Branch and the Rally.

I would like to acknowledge the work done by our Committee in putting this Rally programme together. We are trying a few things this year and we hope everybody, young and not so young, will find a outlet for their talents and something to enjoy.

(Caption under the photograph: Andy Roche - Commodore 2002)

A special word of thanks is due to our sponsors whose continuing support is essential to the success of the Rally.

The Rally is a great opportunity to participate, to learn about boating and water safety, to renew friendships and make new ones. I am looking forward to meeting you all during the week. I hope you will get the maximum enjoyment from the Rally and that you will participate in all the events - both the competitions and the social events.

Have a great time and as we enjoy ourselves let us continue to show the same consideration to other water users as is the tradition of this Rally.

Go n'éirí go geal libh le linn na seachtaine.

Andy Roche
