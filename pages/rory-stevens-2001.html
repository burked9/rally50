Rally 26 (2001): Rory Stevens - Millennium Memories

Millennium Memories

I remember Mat Gallagher's face when I replied to his request for this article, "what article?" and, "did I say I would write an article?," and this was one week before going to press. The long finger I had put this article on was about to be shoved very firmly and very precisely up my stern gland in Mat's own and inimitable fashion.

So, what to write about, would I mention Eamon Egan's ambulance ride to hospital strapped to a stretcher, with suspected neck injuries, and the subsequent relief of all the Egan family, rally entrants and friends when the news came back, only serious bruising but no permanent damage done. Eamon being Eamon still from time to time sleeps in an upright position, being the position he slipped from on Rally 2000, however, Martina now insists that he is well tied on the bow and stern, and sprung around the middle with one of his own well known midships springs.

Toss Quinn and Tom Moore have both gone on courses since Rally 2000, but as on the rally they both ended up on the same one and spent a great day together in Dublin.

It was an absolute pleasure to be involved in the running of the combined North and South event and the two committees worked very well together and always in a good humoured yet focused way, willingly led by our hard working chairman Joe Tracey. I remember the morning committee meetings held on the Iron Lung in Athlone, with Sean chairing, to the tempo of him tapping the top of his boiled egg. The day usually unfolded as the egg did, either very hard or very soft.

There were a few mistakes made, non more so than by myself, the band weren't loud enough for a start, they weren't Jazzy enough and they were paid. Joking apart the lads played brilliantly that night, I just booked them for the wrong venue.

I hope we all made some new friends on Rally 2000, I know I did, and still have them aren't those fleas a b------d to get rid of.

Sitting here in the middle of June 2001, writing this article I cannot believe that Paul Kenny, who was so full of energy and good humour, has been taken from us. Paul represented everything positive in a rally entrant, keen, friendly and determined to ensure that enjoyment was had by all. Paul made many good friends on Lough Derg in the two short years he spent with us, and he will be greatly missed by all.

I would like to wish Michelle, Commodore 2001 and her committee the best for 2001. I know she will do a brilliant job with her brand new husband, lady Commodore Joe.

Rory.
