Rally Magazine 27 (2002): The Roche

First published in Rally Magazine 27 (2002)

The ~~Roach~~, Roche

The Roche is a pleasant looking fish if a bit grey around the gills. It awakes early(ish) in the morning and swims to the surface open mouthed trying to catch as much food as possible. He even steals food from the smaller fish around him! He then swims around looking for something interesting to do for the day - He spies some other fish having a game of football - Indeed our favourite little fish likes any kind of sport where balls of any shape or size are involved. He will spend most of his time watching the game with his mouth open either shouting at the referee or telling anyone who cares to listen his views on the abilities or lack thereof of all the fish playing.

After the game he is exhausted and parched he swims about looking for a glass or two of squid juice. Much later he flounders to the surface mouth open in search of more food. The male Roche tend to eat a little more than is necessary for the day's activities. We believe the additional calories are stored around the mid-section for winter hibernation. Roche are creatures of habit and they tend to like their own comfortable resting place for the evening and before they know it, they are fast asleep again with - yes, you guessed it - their mouths open as if they are snoring.

Cliona Roche
