"That Bates Banagher"

Featured in Rally 17 Magazine

"THAT BATES BANAGHER"

Now you all heard the expression "That 'Bates' Banagher" but did you know where the expression came from? There are many versions but I will tell you the one and only true version. It happened a long time ago. There was a young "gasun" in Banagher called Sean Croughan. Now Sean loved fishing and spent all the time that he could down on the banks of the River Shannon "Fishing". He didn't have a rod as such just a branch of a tree, a homespun line and a home made hook. He experimented with various baits, worms, flies, insects, berries, etc. and became an expert at catching all types of fish. They were poor farmers and Sean's catch of fish was very welcome to supplement the meagre diet. His mother swopped the surplus fish for eggs etc.

One day Sean was fishing when he got a bite. He couldn't hold the rod, it was pulled out of his hand and disappeared. He went home crying. His father made him another stronger rod, line and hook and Sean continued fishing. About a week passed and while Sean was fishing with some of his friends he got another strong bite but this time he held tight and his friends helped him. Nevertheless they were all pulled into the river and the rod line and hook disappeared again. Word soon spread about the big fish that Sean nearly caught and several of the villagers tried to catch the fish but it ended the same. Rods etc were pulled into the river and serveral were nearly drowned. Some had seen the fish and described it as being a monster "POOKA PIKE". Sean got his father to make him an extra strong line and hook. Sean tied a frog to the hook and the end of the line to a big elder tree that was growing beside the river. His friends watched as he let the frog into the stream. The frog swam down stream and as they watched it there was a monsterous splash and the frog disappeared. The strong line jerked like a mad thing. They all watched in fear and awe. The line jerked, the tree shook so much that all the leaves fell off it and they never grew again. The water was churned into foam. Soon the whole village of Banagher was down watching. Eventually they took turns at watching. It went on for a week before the jerking of the line began to ease off.

Next day Sean's father got two of the strongest horses in the village and he shackled them together. When the line became slack he quickly cut and tied it on to the harness of the horses. He led the two horses slowly up the bank of the river through a field until the monsterhead of the "POOKA PIKE" was above water on the land. Then with a shout he urged the two horses into a run and the huge fish was hauled out of the water onto the land and do you know that the water in the River Shannon fell SIX INCHES when the fish was hauled out of the water. Crowds came from far and near to view the huge fish. The only entrance to the field was down a "boreen" and to this day this boreen is called Pooka Lane. The pike was killed and two men using a crosscut saw cut him into pieces and each family in the village had enough pike to make two day's meals. Now Sean did a peculiar thing, he put his hand into the carcass and pulled the pike's heart out. He washed it and asked his mother to cook it for him. When it was cooked Sean ate it all and slept soundly that night.

After all the excitement over the Pooka Pike died down Sean took up fishing again but as time passed Sean found he couldn't catch any fish at all. He could see all the fish in the water but no matter what he did no fish would take the bait. After a while he decided to show his friend where to fish on condition that they gave him a share of the catch. This worked quite well and he continued showing others where to fish on agreeing to share the catch. As Sean grew up he saved every penny he got and when he had his fare saved he headed off to the U.S.A. When Sean arrived in the U.S.A. Sean went to the nearest large bank and asked to see the manager. He explained to the manager that he had the Pooka gift. That he could see all the fish in the water and if the bank manager would lend him the money to buy a large boat, he could take fishing parties out and show them where to fish when they would catch many fish they would come again and tell their friends and he would be able to pay him back. As it so happened the bank manager's hobby was fishing and he had a large boat himself, so he arranged to take Sean out for a day's fishing as he was a bit doubtful about Sean's Pooka Gift.

They went out fishing for the day and sure enough Sean showed him where to fish and soon the boat was full of fish. He was amazed. He came to an agreement with Sean about the boat and within a few years they both were millionaires and Sean's fame had spread. Sean hadn't forgotten his friends and family at home in Banagher and he shared his good fortune with them. He sent the fare to his fishing friend and when he came out Sean made him Captain of the fishing boat. His name was Finn MacMurragh. Now you all heard of "JAWS" well there was really a "JAWS". On the beach at Florida this hugh fish suddenly appeared and going along the beach with his mouth wide open he swallowed a dozen bathers. There was consternation. The Navy was called out but they couldn't kill the fish as they couldn't get near the beach. Everything was tried with no success. Eventually Sean was sent for and asked to get the fish and name his own price. Sean and Finn took a large fishing boat to the scene and soon located the fish near the beach. He was a huge shark as big as a whale. He was resting on the bottom. Sean had a special Triple Hook made and attached to a Hawser which was attached to an earth mover on the beach. He baited the hook with fresh fish and dropped it down in front of the huge shark. Sure enough he took the bait and Sean gave the signal. The earth mover started up and slowly moved up the beach. The hawser came taut and slowly the fish was pulled towards the shore. Just as its monsterous head broke water there was a gasp of fear and awe from hundreds of people watching. At that moment the fish went wild and twisting and treshing his tail he jumped and jerked and all of a sudden the hawser broke and away he swam back into the water. There was a loud gasp of disappointment from all. Sean and Finn were very disappointed and they had a confab about it. They had another heavier hawser and triple hook made and assembled as before but this time Sean soaked the fish bait in a pot full of a liquid he had sent over to him from Banagher. When he dropped the bait down in front of the fish the next time he watched the fish take the bait but he waited all day to let the liquid take effect before giving the signal to haul away. This time the earth mover had no bother hauling the fish out. When the fish was clear out of the water a naval officer shot it dead and a great sigh of relief went up. A man came up to Sean all excited and said "Do you know Sir that the water went down twelve inches when the fish was hauled out?". At that Sean turned to his friend Fin and said "Well Finn, That Bates Banagher!!".

Eric Timon
