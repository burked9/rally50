The Journey to Lough Derg Rally 1

03 February 2026 in Articles

Extracted from the Chronology of the IWAI by Ruth Delany

Year Event/Milestone
1949 I.W.A. formed in England. Green and Silver published by Lionel Thomas Caswall Rolt.
1953 Meeting held at 58 Seafield Road. Decision to form an Irish I.W.A..
1954 Inaugural meeting held in Shelbourne Hotel attended by over 200 people. The Tanaiste, William Norton, and the chairman of CIE Mr. Courtney, taken on a trip on the Shannon and persuaded to inaugurate a passenger service.
1955 CIE passenger service inaugurated. Branches formed at Killaloe, Nenagh, Carlow and Mullingar.
1959 CIE withdrew the canal boats except for a few boats which continued to carry for Guinness into 1960 while a new store in Limerick was being completed.
1960 First boat hire firms started for the Shannon by O'Brien-Kennedys at Shannon Harbour and Dick McGarry at Roosky.
1961 First Annual Shannon Rally with 70 boats taking part - with free fuel.
1962 First Spring Rally on the Barrow.
1963 IWAI's suggestion of a jetty at Clonmacnois is implemented.
1970 Regular IWAI newsletters commenced.
1970 Lough Derg Branch formed embracing moribund branches at Killaloe, Nenagh and Portumna.
1971 First annual Shannon Harbour Canal Boat Rally.
1975 First annual Lough Derg rally held.
